San Antonio’s
3 Most Popular Flooring Choices

Scratches, fading, stains, and regret—that’s what happens when you choose the wrong flooring. San Antonio’s heat, humidity, and daily wear demand more. That’s why we’ve narrowed it down to the top 3 flooring choices built to last in Texas homes— so you get durability, style, and zero regrets.

Waterproof Luxury Vinyl Plank (LVP) – The Floor That Can Handle Anything

  • 100% Waterproof – No warping from spills, humidity, or pet accidents
  • Scratch-Resistant – Perfect for homes with kids, pets, and heavy foot traffic
  •  Low Maintenance – No staining, easy to clean, and won’t fade in the sun
  • Soft Underfoot – More comfortable than tile and hardwood

Best for: Kitchens, bathrooms, high-traffic areas, and homes with pets or kids

Engineered Hardwood – Real Wood Without the Headaches

  • Stronger Than Solid Hardwood – Resists warping, cracking, and shrinking in San Antonio’s humidity
  •  Scratch & Dent Resistant – Built to handle heavy furniture and foot traffic
  • UV Protection – Won’t fade from direct sunlight like traditional hardwood
  • Adds Value to Your Home – A smart investment that looks and feels premium

Best for: Living rooms, bedrooms, and upscale spaces

Large Format Porcelain Tile – Built to Handle Heat, Water & Heavy Use

  • Waterproof & Stain-Proof – No worries about spills, moisture, or messes
  • UV-Resistant – Won’t fade or discolor from sunlight exposure
  • Fewer Grout Lines = Less Maintenance – Easier to clean, less scrubbing
  • Feels Cool Underfoot – Perfect for Texas summers

Best for: Kitchens, bathrooms, patios, and high-traffic areas
